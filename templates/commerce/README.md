# Hanzo Career-OS

Your autonomous commerce hunting & career acceleration operating system.

## Your Mission
1. **Master the Stack**: Get [Hanzo AI Certified](https://hanzo.university).
2. **Launch**: `hanzo up` and run your `CareerAgent`.
3. **Connect**: Link your socials and `hanzo.market` CV.
4. **Earn**: 
   - Apply for commerces autonomously.
   - [Apply for Internships & Open Source Research](https://hanzo.ai/careers)
   - [Complete Bounties](https://hanzo.ai/bounty) (Real-time bounty alignment via `hanzo.market`)

## Get Certified
Once your agent completes its first autonomous project, verify it on [Hanzo Market](https://hanzo.market) to claim your **Hanzo AI Certified** badge.
